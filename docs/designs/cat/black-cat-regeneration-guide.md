# Black Cat Regeneration Guide

`black_cat_stalk_v5.png` is the approved visual master for the black-cat set.
For future assets, reference it and change only the requested pose. Generate a
new versioned file first; never overwrite an approved asset before review.

## Locked generation rule

- Reference `black_cat_stalk_v5.png` for every black-cat generation.
- Change only the requested pose: paw placement, body gesture, head direction,
  and tail gesture.
- Preserve the approved face, body proportions, expression, line weight,
  watercolor treatment, scale, framing, and transparent background.
- Keep the v5 silhouette subtly sleek: a lightly slimmer torso and hip width,
  longer-looking limbs, and a gently narrower cheek and muzzle shape. Do not
  make the kitten skinny, angular, or adult-looking.
- Keep the coat a single deep charcoal-black color. Use only faint, same-family
  translucent shading to separate the chin, belly, legs, tail, and body.
- Keep contours near-black charcoal, softly painted, and darker than the coat.
  Never use warm brown, blue, or thick ink outlines.
- Keep irises a muted honey-gold yellow with dark pupils and small white
  highlights. Never use green, neon yellow, orange, or amber eyes.
- Use exactly three delicate, tapered whiskers per side: soft cool off-white or
  light gray, medium-fine in weight, and never thick white ribbons.

## Style lock

- Cute black kitten with a rounded head, large eyes, soft pink inner ears, a
  small dark charcoal-brown nose, tiny friendly mouth, and fine fanned whiskers.
- Hand-painted children's-book watercolor: fine paper grain inside the pigment,
  softly uneven edges, restrained translucent layering, and subtle form shading.
- The black coat must remain readable against a transparent background through
  minimal watercolor value shifts, not through gray patches or bright highlights.
- Whiskers are lightly visible against black fur but must not dominate the face.
- Isolated full-body PNG with a genuinely transparent background.

## Review checklist

1. Compare coat darkness, eye color, and whisker weight against
   `black_cat_stalk_v5.png`.
2. Confirm there are exactly three fine, tapered whiskers per side.
3. Confirm the coat is unified charcoal-black with only minimal same-family
   shading, while body parts remain readable.
4. Confirm a transparent background and no clipped ears, paws, whiskers, or
   tail.
5. Keep approved files untouched; promote a new version only after review.
