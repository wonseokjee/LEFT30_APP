# Cream Longhair Cat Regeneration Guide

`cream_cat_tail_up_v1.png` is the approved visual master for the cream
longhair-cat set. For future assets, reference it and change only the requested
pose. Generate a new versioned file first; never overwrite an approved asset
before review.

## Locked generation rule

- Reference `cream_cat_tail_up_v1.png` for every cream longhair-cat generation.
- Change only the requested pose: paw placement, body gesture, head direction,
  and tail gesture.
- Preserve the approved face, full longhair silhouette, fur density, tuft
  placement, expression, line weight, watercolor treatment, scale, framing,
  and genuinely transparent background.
- The coat must stay richly fluffy and continuous around the cheek ruff, chest,
  belly, legs, tail base, and raised plume tail. Do not simplify, merge, remove,
  enlarge, or sparsely space the individual visible longhair tufts.
- Keep the creamy ivory base, pale beige shadows, peach-tan accents, warm brown
  eyes, pink inner ears, pink nose, and fine warm-brown whiskers.
- Keep the warm brown, softly variable contour pigment. Do not use black, gray,
  or harsh continuous outlines.
- For a tail-wag or raised-tail pose, retain the master's small curved motion
  accents near the tail; omit them for a still-tail pose.

## Style lock

- Cute, plush cream long-haired kitten with a broad fluffy cheek ruff, full
  chest and belly, fluffy legs, and a large upturned plume tail.
- Large warm brown eyes with dark pupils and small white highlights; dusty pink
  triangular nose; tiny gentle mouth; fine fanned whiskers.
- Soft children's-book watercolor with a fine paper-grain texture, layered
  creamy pigment, warm peach-beige shadow shapes, and gently uneven painted
  edges.
- The kitten should feel soft, full, and expressive, not sleek, short-haired,
  sparse, or realistically hairy.
- Isolated full-body PNG with a genuinely transparent background.

Use this after the pose line for every asset:

```text

white highlights; pink triangular nose; tiny gentle mouth; soft pink inner ears;
and fine warm-brown whiskers. Keep the cheek ruff, chest, belly, legs, tail base,

Style/medium: hand-painted children's-book watercolor matching

Color palette: creamy ivory base fur, pale warm-beige shadows, and restrained
peach-tan accents. Warm brown eyes, pink ears and nose.
Scene/backdrop: no scene; genuinely transparent background.
Constraints: preserve the master character design, coat fullness, tuft density,
watercolor treatment, and framing; change only the pose line. Full body in

Avoid: sparse or simplified fur, large blank coat areas, merged tufts, short
fur, realistic individual hairs, a sleek silhouette, dark/gray/black outlines,
blue-gray shadows, flat vector art, background, watermark.
```

## Review checklist

1. Compare fur fullness, tuft density, and tail plume volume against
   `cream_cat_tail_up_v1.png`.
2. Confirm no empty-looking gaps appear in the cheek ruff, chest, belly, leg,
   or tail fur.
3. Confirm the cream, beige, peach, pink, and warm-brown palette matches the
   approved master.
4. Confirm a transparent background and no clipped ears, paws, whiskers, or
   tail.
5. Keep approved files untouched; promote a new version only after review.
