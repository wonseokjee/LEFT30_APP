# White Cat Regeneration Guide

`white_cat_stalk_v4.png` is the approved visual master for the white-cat set.
For future assets, reference it and change only the requested pose. Generate a
new versioned file first; never overwrite an approved asset before review.

## Locked generation rule

- Reference `white_cat_stalk_v4.png` for every white-cat generation.
- Change only the requested pose: paw placement, body gesture, head direction,
  and tail gesture.
- Preserve the approved face, body proportions, expression, line weight,
  watercolor treatment, scale, framing, and truly transparent background.
- Keep the coat clean snowy white with only minimal pale cool-gray watercolor
  shadows beneath the chin, belly, legs, and tail. Do not add cream patches,
  gray markings, or strong shadow bands.
- Keep outer and interior contours soft medium cool gray, always darker than
  the coat but never black, brown, or beige.
- Keep irises a clear natural medium sky blue with a subtle deeper blue-gray
  rim, dark pupils, and small white highlights. Do not use yellow, green, teal,
  purple, neon blue, or navy eyes.
- Keep the soft pink nose, pink inner ears, and three fine cool-light-gray
  whiskers per side.

## Style lock

- Cute white kitten with a rounded head, large blue eyes, pink triangular nose,
  tiny friendly mouth, and delicate fanned whiskers.
- Hand-painted children's-book watercolor with fine paper grain inside the
  paint, softly uneven painted edges, sparse translucent layering, and very
  restrained cool-gray form shading.
- The white coat must stay bright and clean while remaining readable against a
  transparent background through subtle paint texture and pale shadowing.
- Isolated full-body PNG with a genuinely transparent background.

## Shared prompt

Use this after the pose line for every asset:

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: transparent game character asset
Subject: a single cute white kitten, full body, with the exact character design,
face and body proportions of white_cat_stalk_v4.png. Large clear medium sky-blue
eyes with a subtle deeper blue-gray iris rim, dark pupils, and small white
highlights; soft pink inner ears; a small pink triangular nose; a tiny friendly
mouth; and exactly three fine cool-light-gray whiskers per side.
Style/medium: hand-painted children's-book watercolor matching
white_cat_stalk_v4.png, with fine paper grain within the pigment, soft uneven
painted edges, sparse translucent layering, and gentle cool-gray contours.
Color palette: clean snowy-white coat with only minimal pale cool-gray
transparent shadows to separate the chin, belly, legs, and tail. Contours are
soft medium cool gray. Irises are natural medium sky blue.
Scene/backdrop: no scene; genuinely transparent background.
Constraints: preserve the master character design, line weight, watercolor
treatment, framing, and transparent background; change only the pose line.
Full body in frame; no text, props, or ground shadow.
Avoid: cream patches, gray markings, strong gray shadows, black/brown/beige
outlines, yellow/green/teal/purple/neon-blue/navy eyes, thick white whiskers,
realistic fur, vector art, background, watermark.
```

## Review checklist

1. Compare coat brightness, eye color, and contour color against
   `white_cat_stalk_v4.png`.
2. Confirm the coat is clean white with only minimal pale cool-gray shading.
3. Confirm irises are the approved medium sky blue and the nose is soft pink.
4. Confirm a transparent background and no clipped ears, paws, whiskers, or
   tail.
5. Keep approved files untouched; promote a new version only after review.
