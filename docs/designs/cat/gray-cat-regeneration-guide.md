# Gray Cat Regeneration Guide

`gray_cat_stalk_v12.png` is the approved visual master for the gray-cat set.
For future assets, use it as the image reference and change only the requested
pose. Generate a new versioned file first; do not overwrite an approved asset
until it has been reviewed.

## Locked generation rule

- Reference `gray_cat_stalk_v12.png` for every gray-cat generation.
- Change only the requested pose: paw placement, body gesture, head direction,
  and tail gesture.
- Preserve its face, body proportions, line weight, watercolor treatment,
  illustration scale, framing, and truly transparent background.
- Keep the coat as one unified muted cool-gray color. Use only very subtle
  same-gray, low-opacity shading for form; do not add highlight bands, patches,
  stripes, or separate fur-color regions.
- Keep all outer and interior contours darker than the coat in a soft
  charcoal-blue-gray. They must never be brown, taupe, or black.
- Keep the irises as the quiet, desaturated gray-olive gold in the approved
  master. Do not use bright yellow, orange, or amber eyes.

## Style lock

- Cute, slightly crouched gray kitten with a gently rounded head, large eyes,
  small pink triangular nose, tiny friendly mouth, and long fine white whiskers.
- Hand-painted children's-book watercolor with fine paper grain visible within
  the pigment, softly uneven paint edges, and sparse translucent layering.
- Coat color: medium muted cool gray with a very slight blue undertone. It must
  read as gray, never warm brown/beige and never saturated blue.
- Coat rendering: one paint color across head, body, tail, and legs; only
  minimal same-color transparent shading under the chin, belly, legs, and tail
  attachment.
- Outline: darker charcoal-blue-gray watercolor pigment, softly variable and
  slightly broken rather than a thick continuous ink stroke.
- Ear interiors are soft pink; pupils are dark with small white highlights.
- Isolated full-body PNG with a genuinely transparent background.

## Shared prompt

Use this after the pose line for every asset:

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: transparent game character asset
Subject: a single cute gray kitten, full body, with the exact character design,
face and body proportions of gray_cat_stalk_v12.png. Large quiet gray-olive-gold
eyes with dark pupils and small white highlights; soft pink inner ears; a small
pink triangular nose; a tiny friendly mouth; and long fine white whiskers.
Style/medium: hand-painted children's-book watercolor matching
gray_cat_stalk_v12.png, with fine paper grain within the pigment, soft uneven
painted edges, sparse translucent layering, and gently broken watercolor
contours.
Color palette: one unified medium muted cool-gray coat with a slight blue
undertone. Use only minimal same-gray transparent form shadows. Outer and
interior contours are darker charcoal-blue-gray. Keep the irises muted
gray-olive gold; never bright yellow, orange, or amber.
Scene/backdrop: no scene; genuinely transparent background.
Constraints: preserve the master character design, line weight, watercolor
treatment, framing, and transparent background; change only the pose line.
Full body in frame; no text, props, or ground shadow.
Avoid: brown, beige, taupe, or saturated-blue fur; multi-color fur layers;
highlight bands; patches; stripes; bright yellow/orange eyes; black or brown
outlines; thick sharp outlines; realistic fur; vector art; background; watermark.
```

## Review checklist

1. Compare coat hue, contour color, and iris color against
   `gray_cat_stalk_v12.png`.
2. Confirm the coat remains a single cool-gray color with only minimal
   same-color shading.
3. Confirm contours are darker charcoal-blue-gray, never warm brown.
4. Confirm the PNG background is transparent and ears, paws, whiskers, and tail
   are not clipped.
5. Preserve approved files and promote a new version only after visual review.
